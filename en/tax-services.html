Before you begin

Documents you may need

The documents required may vary according to your tax situation. These are some of the most common:

  • Identification document
  • Social Security number or ITIN, as applicable
  • Spouse’s information
  • Dependents’ information
  • Form W-2
  • Forms 1099
  • Documents related to other income received during the year
  • Expenses related to self-employment or business activity
  • Tax documents received during the year
  • Information related to applicable credits or deductions
  • Prior-year tax return, when needed

Required documents may vary in each case. During the process, we will tell you what information we need to review.

Frequently asked questions

What documents do I need to prepare my return?+

It depends on your tax situation. Identification, income documents such as W-2 or 1099 forms, dependents’ information, and other documents related to your income, expenses, credits, or applicable deductions are generally required.

Do you prepare returns for W-2 employees?+

Yes. Smart Taxes prepares returns for employees who receive W-2 forms and reviews the required information before preparing the return.

Do you serve independent contractors and people who receive 1099 forms?+

Yes. We serve independent contractors, self-employed workers, and people who receive different types of 1099 forms.

Can you help me organize income and expenses from my activity?+

Yes. We can help you organize and review information related to your activity’s income and expenses before preparing your return.

Do you offer virtual tax preparation?+

Yes. Smart Taxes offers virtual service for clients located across the United States.

Do you offer in-person service?+

Yes. In-person service is available in Georgia, and we also offer virtual services for clients across the United States.

Can you serve me in English or Spanish?+

Yes. Our team can serve clients in both English and Spanish.
