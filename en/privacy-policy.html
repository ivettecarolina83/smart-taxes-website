Information and privacy

Privacy Policy

This page explains in clear language what information Smart Taxes receives through its website and how it is used.

Last updated: July 24, 2026

1. Scope of this policy

This policy describes how Smart Taxes collects, uses, and protects information voluntarily provided through this website, especially through the consultation request form.

2. Information we collect

The form may request a first and last name, phone number, email address, preferred contact method, service of interest, and a brief description of the inquiry. It also records the language and website origin of the request.

Do not send Social Security numbers, ITINs, banking information, passwords, tax returns, tax documents, or other confidential information through this form.

The website does not allow or request tax document uploads. Its form is used only to establish initial contact. After an in-person or virtual consultation, Smart Taxes will identify the documents needed and the protected channel to use.

If Smart Taxes later authorizes email transmission, sensitive information should not appear in the subject line or message body. Documents should be encrypted or protected with a strong password, and the password should be communicated through a method other than email.

3. How we use information

We use the information to receive the request, understand the general need described, respond to the person, and coordinate a possible conversation about Smart Taxes services. The form is not used to prepare or file a tax return.

4. Services used to receive requests

Form information is transmitted through an HTTPS connection to Google Apps Script and delivered to the Gmail account used by Smart Taxes. The website is hosted by Hostinger and its code is managed through GitHub. These providers operate under their own policies and terms.

5. Website technical information

When someone visits the website, the hosting provider may automatically record basic technical information such as the IP address, browser or device type, access date and time, and pages viewed. This information may be used to operate, maintain, and protect the website. Smart Taxes does not use these logs to prepare tax returns or to identify visitors for tax purposes.

6. External links and social media

The website includes links to social media and other external platforms. When someone visits those services, information collection and handling are governed by each platform's privacy policy. Smart Taxes does not control their practices or the information a person chooses to provide to them.

7. When we share information

Smart Taxes does not sell information submitted through the form. Access may be provided to preparers or service providers who need the information to address the request, operate the form, host the website, or comply with a valid legal obligation.

8. Retention and security

Information is retained for the time reasonably necessary to address the request, maintain related records, and meet applicable obligations. Smart Taxes seeks to use reasonable administrative and technical measures, but no email or Internet transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices

You may request information about the data you submitted, ask for a correction, or request deletion where appropriate. Contact smarttaxes2@gmail.com. Smart Taxes may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Children's privacy

This form is not directed to minors submitting personal information on their own. A responsible adult who believes a minor provided information may contact Smart Taxes to request review or deletion.

11. Changes to this policy

Smart Taxes may update this policy when the form, service providers, or information-handling practices change. The updated date will appear at the beginning of this page.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy or information submitted through the website, email smarttaxes2@gmail.com.

Informational notice: This policy describes the website's current practices. It does not claim to have been prepared or approved by an attorney.
